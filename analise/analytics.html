Reading guide

Why some states are not shown

Publication coverage

NSW, VIC, QLD and NT do not publish occupation-level invitation results. TAS publishes totals only. SA publishes invitation counts by ANZSCO sub-major group, not scores, so it is outside this score workspace.

Published levels

Federal and WA evidence reaches occupation level. ACT publishes at unit-group level. SA publishes counts at sub-major level. A broader level must not be presented as a six-digit occupation result.

Different migration stages

Allocations, nominations, invitations and visa grants are different measures. None can safely substitute for another, and an allocation is not an invitation or a grant.

What each jurisdiction actually publishes

Active cards contain usable score outcomes and their next activity. Dimmed cards have no score data in this workspace.

Forecast rule: WA’s 30 December date is a monitoring horizon inferred from its irregular historical publication cadence. It is not an announced invitation date. “Awaiting” means no defensible next-round date has been published.
